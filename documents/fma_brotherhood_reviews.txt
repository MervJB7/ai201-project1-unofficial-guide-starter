Full Metal Alchemist: Brotherhood Review: 
 
Review: 1, Username: tazillo, Comment: Calls Brotherhood simply EPIC and a faithful manga adaptation that dispels fears of spoiling the franchise. Praises the fast-but-effective pacing, crisp animation, excellent voice acting, the deep cast including new Xingese characters, and the total lack of filler. A 10, recommended for newcomers and veterans alike.

Review: 2, Username: ChrissyKay, Comment: Pushes back on the masterpiece label, arguing the show is very good but over-hyped. Praises the immersive world, perfect sound, and superb antagonists, but faults the clunky exposition, repetitive gags, and characters who are plot devices defined by trauma rather than personality. Story 8, sound 10, character 6, overall 7.

Review: 3, Username: Archaeon, Comment: A measured take placing Brotherhood as equal to the 2003 series rather than superior. Notes it's a more faithful, more typically shonen adaptation that "de-humanizes" the homunculi and adopts a lighter tone, but praises the wealth of characters, improved production, and cathartic ending. Rates it 9.

Review: 4, Username: Monkey-King, Comment: Argues both Brotherhood and its manga don't work because of a mistranslation of tonal nuance — dark, adult themes constantly undercut by side-gags and quirky commercial transitions. Praises the exquisite dialogue and character writing but says the disjointed tone makes it impossible to take seriously. Rates it 5.

Review: 5, Username: bakababe, Comment: Can't even rewatch the original now. Calls it a no-filler anime that gets straight to the point with beautiful animation and a faithful portrayal of the manga, with stronger emotions and character connections that drag you in within the first five minutes. A spectacular 10 and a definite must-watch.

Review: 6, Username: ryuu_zer0, Comment: Holds it up as a prime example of adapting a manga the right way, unlike the original which spiraled after diverging. Praises the breakneck-then-appropriate pacing, the manga-faithful art, the diverse and substantial cast, and an improved soundtrack that grows over time. Rates it 9.

Review: 7, Username: BaconKingpin, Comment: Disputes the masterpiece reputation, saying that following the manga doesn't make an anime good. Finds the story devolves into a generic save-the-world-from-a-god-complex-villain plot with shallow homunculi, weak character development, and overdramatic cliche dialogue, while praising the animation, dub, and openings. Rates it 4.

Review: 8, Username: ggultra2764, Comment: Argues those calling the 2003 series irrelevant are ridiculous, since the two have completely different takes. Praises the expanded world-building and fleshing-out of underdeveloped characters but faults the rushed early pacing, mood-killing comedy, shallow black-and-white morality, and contrived deus ex machina ending. Rates it 6.

Review: 9, Username: Reimei-Chan, Comment: Rejects the idea that you must pick between this and the original, calling both masterpieces on entertainment and artistic levels. Awards perfect 10s across story, art, sound, characters, and enjoyment, singling out the fantastic ending, the well-animated action, the acclaimed dub, and characters like Roy Mustang and Maes Hughes.

Review: 10, Username: StickyWizard, Comment: Calls it a shining beacon among modern shonen and arguably the most well-rounded of the genre, consistently competent in every category. Praises the excellently paced story exploring alchemy's moral issues, the huge relevant cast, the clever tactics-based fights, and the superb dub and music. Rates it 10.
