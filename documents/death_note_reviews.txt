Death Note Review:

Review: 1, Username: Xinil, Comment: Manga is infinitely better than the anime, but it should have ended at chapter 58/59 — the second half is a thrown-together mess. The first half is ridiculously good with brilliant story and alive, detailed characters, so it couldn't get a bad score.

Review: 2, Username: Polyphemus, Comment: Two genius "Mary Sue" pretty boys with impossible foresight, a plot driven by coincidences and improbabilities, and a too-perfect protagonist whose flawlessness kills any development. Intriguing concept but flawed execution; recommends only the first half, and even that isn't as great as people claim.

Review: 3, Username: Neferium, Comment: A perfect manga of suspense and thriller with a rich storyline and standout characters. Follows Light Yagami's killing spree to create a perfect world and outsmart his foes — and you'll never guess the ending.

Review: 4, Username: AironicallyHuman, Comment: A worldwide hit where words, not fists, are weapons. The first 34-36 chapters are the best manga he's read, with a dip during the Yotsuba section and an uneven second half, but the best parts more than make up for it. Rates the manga 8/10, slightly below the anime.

Review: 5, Username: Sorrow, Comment: His favorite story ever — nearly everything is perfect. Praises the deep plot, symbolic characters, and religious symbolism, and lists the many philosophical questions about justice, good, and evil that the manga raises. A perfect 10/10.

Review: 6, Username: exabyte, Comment: Light picks up the Death Note and goes on a killing spree; L steps in for a battle of wits, then Near and Mello arrive as new protagonists in the second arc. To find out how it ends, read the manga.

Review: 7, Username: miss-makai, Comment: His first manga read fully and still his all-time favorite. Loves the incredible story, the time jumps, and the complex L–Light relationship, though he didn't much like Misa. Great art that gives each character their own look; a true classic worth rereading.

Review: 8, Username: Dietrologia, Comment: One of the biggest series of the 2000s, with a fascinating hook and excellent artwork, but the characters are mostly lackluster. Light is a petulant sociopath with no real nuance and L is the best of the cast; the moral ambiguity is overstated, leaving it a 6/10 thriller.

Review: 9, Username: KaiserSoul, Comment: His first review and a life-changing series. Rates story 9, art 8, characters 15/10, and enjoyment 10. Loves Light as a Satan-like fallen protagonist and L as the strange, entertaining genius, knocking off points only for sidelining characters like Misa near the end.

Review: 10, Username: theaustincritic, Comment: A riveting masterpiece he calls the greatest manga/anime in the world, praising the superb writing, beautiful art, and addicting structure. Notes it's a serious, adult, R-rated anti-hero story that cleverly tackles capital punishment without alienating viewers. 10/10.
