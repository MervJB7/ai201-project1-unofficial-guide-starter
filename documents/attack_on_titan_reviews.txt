Attack on Titan Review. Also known as AOT, AoT or aot:

Review: 1, Username: Sorrowful, Comment: Calls it one of the greatest anime ever made, popular for good reason. Praises the captivating story of humanity confined behind walls against man-eating Titans, the diverse and relatable characters, the stunning art and 3D maneuver gear, and the epic German-flavored soundtrack. A perfect 10.

Review: 2, Username: Gladius650, Comment: Argues SNK is misunderstood because it's a psychological work, not empty violence. It shows the darkest sides of the human psyche, with no fearless heroes — only people fighting their own fear — and the real enemies are human fear and the walls themselves. A masterpiece, 10/10.

Review: 3, Username: SonDavid, Comment: Frames AoT as a landmark on par with Gundam, DBZ, Evangelion, and FMA, calling it the best anime in 15 years. Gushes over the OST, directing, characters, OP/ED sequences, animation, and original story, awarding an over-the-top 101/100.

Review: 4, Username: Kerma_, Comment: Sees it as the perfect mainstream gateway anime but flawed on reflection. The pacing whiplashes between thrilling action and tedious bickering, and the plot/character development is largely an illusion. Compares it to a Michael Bay summer blockbuster; story 4, art 9, sound 10, character 3, overall 5.

Review: 5, Username: emberreviews, Comment: Calls it the Game of Thrones of anime, mastering suspense through long arcs and "morale builder" moments with paper-thin plot armor and frequent major deaths. Praises Armin, Mikasa, and Eren, the Wit Studio animation, the FUNimation dub, and Sawano's soundtrack. Rates it 9 with one flaw of occasional over-dramatics.

Review: 6, Username: Wandering-Otaku, Comment: An astounding setting with an amazing first three episodes that becomes disappointing as the plot turns predictable. Praises the soundtrack as the true beauty but finds the art mediocre and most of the cast unlikable, with only Armin standing out. A 5/10 of squandered potential.

Review: 7, Username: Xiaraith, Comment: A full rhyming-poem review arguing the show is average and over-hyped despite its popularity. Concedes the art and the German-Japanese opening theme are impressive but faults the flat characters, off pacing, dramatized filler, and unanswered Titan questions. Rates it 5.

Review: 8, Username: Shrabster, Comment: Posted as a "Funny" not-recommended review, calling it glorified torture porn that fails to live up to its premise. Faults the bland cookie-cutter cast, one-note angry Eren, flawless Mikasa, 
tonally inconsistent humor, and pacing issues, while granting the production values are good. Rates it 3.

Review: 9, Username: Micsupreeme, Comment: An engaging, intense, action-packed show capable of converting non-anime watchers, with perfectly executed combat and a constant sense of peril and accurate scale of death. Faults the somewhat linear story and repetitive plot-summary intros but agrees it lives up to its reputation. Rates it 8.

Review: 10, Username: Nikolekoleta, Comment: Left her jaw open more than a handful of times, with more deaths than the entire A Song of Ice and Fire series. Praises the genius, terrifying concept of a humanlike villain with no conscience, the dark society and real-world parallels, the 3D Maneuver Gear, and the amazing music. Rates it 9.
